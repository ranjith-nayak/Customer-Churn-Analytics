# Customer Churn Prediction & Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer churn is one of the most critical challenges faced by subscription-based businesses. Understanding why customers leave and identifying at-risk customers can help organizations improve retention strategies and reduce revenue loss.

This project combines **Data Analytics, Machine Learning, and Power BI** to analyze customer behavior, identify churn drivers, and predict future customer churn.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze customer churn patterns and trends.
* Identify key factors contributing to customer attrition.
* Build predictive machine learning models to forecast churn.
* Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard for business stakeholders.
* Generate actionable business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 🤖 Machine Learning Results

### Logistic Regression

* Accuracy: 82.5%

### Random Forest Classifier

* Accuracy: 94.8%

### ROC-AUC Score

* 87.1%

### Top Predictive Features

1. Satisfaction Score
2. Tenure in Months
3. Total Revenue
4. Total Charges
5. Monthly Charges
6. Contract Type

---

## 💡 Key Insights

* Competitor-related issues are the leading cause of customer churn.
* Customers with lower satisfaction scores are more likely to leave.
* Month-to-Month contracts show higher churn rates.
* Customer tenure strongly impacts retention.
* Pricing and service quality significantly influence churn behavior.
